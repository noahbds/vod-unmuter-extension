Twitch VOD Unmuter is ready

Open any Twitch VOD: muted parts play with their sound, as long as Twitch still has it.

What it can and can't do

  • Twitch keeps the original sound of muted parts for only 13 to 32 hours after a stream ends (measured on 81 VODs), then deletes it for good. After that, nothing can bring it back, except audio this extension saved before.
  • So VODs opened within a day of the stream are unmuted, and their sound is saved on this computer so they stay unmuted later. Older VODs usually can't be unmuted.
  • The counter next to the player's ⚙️ and the seek bar colors show what was restored. Hover a red part to see why it's still muted.

Don't miss the next VODs

Add the channels you watch: their new VODs are saved in the background, while Twitch still has the sound, even if you don't open them in time.

Change this any time in the extension's menu (its icon in the toolbar).

Good to know

  • In this browser, saved audio can't be compressed (no AAC encoder): it takes about 1.1 to 2.4 MB per muted minute. The storage limit is in the extension's menu.
  • VODs embedded in other websites and the mobile site (m.twitch.tv) can't be unmuted: Twitch only gives the original sound to www.twitch.tv. The counter offers to open the VOD there.
  • Everything stays in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking.
  • Something wrong? The extension's menu has a status line and a Copy diagnostic report button for a GitHub issue.
